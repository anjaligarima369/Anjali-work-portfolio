How it was done
Step 01
Identify the gap
Used Flipkart Iris to pull category-level GMV data for brands not currently running ads. High GMV with zero ad spend = the pitch writes itself.
Step 02
Build the ROI case
Mapped competitor ad spend, category search volume, and placement fill rates. Showed brands exactly what they were missing by not advertising — with numbers, not opinions.
Step 03
Coordinate the launch
End-to-end onboarding: Billing & AR for credit setup, Finance for postpaid approvals, Operations for creatives, CTAs, and FSNs, Planning for placement strategy and CPC/CPM benchmarks.
Step 04
Convert to PCA
Once a brand was spending on PLA, the next conversation was PCA — Video, Interactive, Rich Banner, Pinned Spotlight. This is how a ₹2L/month account became a ₹10L/month account.
Step 05
Retain and grow
Weekly tracking, monthly reviews, QBRs. Not just "how did the campaign perform" but "here's what we do next quarter to grow your Ads/GMV ratio."
